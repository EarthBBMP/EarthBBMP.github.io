About

More about me

Right now I lead EIC-ARRT, the aerial robotics team at Chula's Engineering Innovator Club, and I'm one of five engineers building a boiler inspection robot for Getabec.

Before that I spent almost two years as a software developer at AIMET, shipping wellness center apps for Chula's medical students. On the research side, I have two papers accepted at NeurIPS 2026 workshops, and our robot placed 6th in the world at RoboCup@Home 2026.

Day to day I work in Python and C++, with ROS 2, PX4 and Gazebo for robots, PyTorch for models, and React, Node.js and Go for the web.

Research

All research

Accepted NeurIPS 2026, New in ML and Global South AI workshops

Still Citing the Old Law? Statutory Knowledge and Temporal Anchoring in Thai Legal Question Answering

A bilingual Thai/English benchmark on four dated amendments to Thai law, used to test whether nine LLMs answer with the current law or the repealed one. 97.7% of the wrong answers came with no sign of uncertainty.

Accepted NeurIPS 2026, New in ML and ICBINB workshops

Any Promoter Will Do: Auditing a Sequence-to-Expression Model on Patient-Derived Data

An audit of a released model that predicts mRNA abundance from promoter sequence, on patient data for 15,370 genes. Swapping, shuffling or scrambling the promoter changes R² by under 0.010.
