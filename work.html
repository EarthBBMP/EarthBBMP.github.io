Robotics Mar 2026 – now Getabec PCL · EIC Chula team

Boiler inspection crawler

A magnetic-wheeled crawler robot that inspects boiler surfaces for dents, scratches and marks. Five of us from EIC Chula are building it under contract for Getabec.

Details
  • Raspberry Pi 5 and Arduino stack with an IMU and a global-shutter camera.
  • Uses photometric stereo imaging to detect and measure surface defects. Currently in bench testing.
  • We demonstrated the working prototype under the Getabec name at ProPak Asia 2026 (BITEC, Bangkok).

Raspberry Pi 5 · Arduino · IMU · photometric stereo
