About.

I'm Suphachat, an engineering student at Chulalongkorn University's International School of Engineering (ISE) in Bangkok. I build robots, AI systems and software, and the goal is the same each time: technology that's useful to people.

Most of my time now goes to robotics. I lead EIC-ARRT, the aerial robotics team at Chula's Engineering Innovator Club, where every mission flies in Gazebo before it flies for real. I'm also one of five engineers building a boiler inspection crawler for Getabec, which we demonstrated at ProPak Asia 2026. In 2026 our EIC-Chula team placed 6th in the world at RoboCup@Home.

I started taking on real work before university: AI research at V89 Technology in 2024, then an MRI project with the Faculty of Medicine and a software developer role at AIMET that lasted almost two years. For two years I also taught C++, Python, data structures and AI fundamentals at Leagues of Code.

Off the clock I play bass guitar and sing, and play basketball, badminton and chess.
